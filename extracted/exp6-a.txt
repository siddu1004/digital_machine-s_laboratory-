REGULATION OF ALTERNATOR BY EMF AND MMF METHODS 
AIM: 
To predetermine the voltage regulation of the given non-salient pole alternator at any 
load by EMF and MMF methods at different power factors and plot percentage voltage 
regulation Vs Power factor curve.

PROCEDURE: 
a) OPEN CIRCUIT TEST 
1. Wire up the circuit as shown in the circuit diagram. 
2. Set the field rheostat of the motor to minimum resistance and that of the alternator to 
maximum resistance position. 
3. The rheostats in the armature circuit of the motor are set for maximum resistance 
position. 
4. The TPST switch is kept open, and the DC motor is started using 3-point starter. 
5. Using the armature rheostats of the motor, the alternator is driven at rated speed if not 
achieved, the resistance in the field rheostat is included. 
6. Keeping the SPST open and ensuring zero field current in the alternator side, the 
residual voltage is noted down. Then the SPST is closed. 
--- Page 2 ---
7. Adjust the field current of the alternator in steps and corresponding to each field 
current, record the terminal voltage on open circuit. 
8. The experiment is repeated until about 120% of the rated voltage is obtained. 
Throughout the experiment, the speed must be maintained at the rated speed value. 
9. Plot the phase value of open circuit voltage against field current to obtain the open 
circuit characteristic curve. 
 
b) SHORT CIRCUIT TEST 
1. Reduce the field excitation of the alternator to the minimum position and open the 
SPST switch. 
2. Close the TPST and adjust the field rheostat of the alternator so that the rated current 
flows through the short-circuited armature winding. 
3. Note down the corresponding field current(Ifsc) 
4. The Short Circuit Characteristic curve is drawn as a straight line passing through the 
points (0,0) and (Ifsc, I) where ‘I’  is the rated current of the alternator. 
c) ARMATURE (STATOR) RESISTANCE MEASUREMENT 
The armature resistance is found by the voltmeter-ammeter method. The effective armature 
resistance may be taken 1.2 times that of obtained value. This is to take the skin effect into 
account.
