LOAD TEST ON THREE-PHASE ALTERNATOR 
AIM: 
 To determine the performance characteristics of three phase alternator by conducting a load test 
on it. 
APPARATUS REQUIRED:  
S.NO APPARATUS RANGE QUANTITY 
1 Rheostat   
2 Rheostat   
3 Rheostat   
4 Ammeter   
5 Ammeter   
6 Voltmeter   
7 Voltmeter   
7 Tachometer   
 
PROCEDURE: 
1. Connections are given as per the circuit diagram. 
2. Ensure that the motor field rheostat is in minimum position, the armature rheostat in 
maximum position and the alternator field rheostat is in maximum position. 
3. The supply is switched ON from the DC side.  
4. The 3-point starter is gradually moved from the OFF to the ON position. 
5. The motor is brought to its rated speed by adjusting its armature and field rheostats. 
6. The terminal voltage of the alternator is brought to its rated voltage by varying the field 
rheostat of the alternator.  
7. Resistive load connected to the alternator is varied in steps from no load till the rated 
current is reached. 
8. Field current, terminal voltage, and load current of the alternator are noted down in 
steps. 
9. In each step, ensure that the machine runs at rated speed. 
10. Now, the resistive load is brought to its initial position. 
11. Then, the alternator field rheostat and motor field rheostat is brought to its initial 
condition.
